Familiar by design

Your files live where they always did.

Open the root of the disk and you see three folders: Applications, System and Users. Not a wall of cryptic names. The rest is hidden away, the way a Mac hides it. Your home is /Users/you.

Programs are folders in /Applications. Delete a folder, the program is gone. Drop an AppImage in, it is installed.

What you get

Everything a switcher needs, nothing they do not.

Drop to install

Put an .appimage, .tar.zst or .pkg into Applications and it installs. Delete it and it uninstalls.

niri + Quickshell

A smooth scrolling window manager with a shell we wrote: launcher, calendar, network, sound, screenshots, power.

One package manager

pkg install, pkg remove, pkg upgrade. That is the whole thing. The engine underneath is hidden.

Black everywhere

One dark theme across GTK and Qt apps, black terminal, black shell, Papirus icons.

A friendly installer

Pick a Wi-Fi network from a list, tick your keyboard layouts, choose a disk. It sets up only the graphics drivers your hardware needs.

Honest handbook

Plain-language guide in English, Russian and German. Changing a keybind takes five lines.

Simple on purpose

Three commands. That is the manual.

Install, remove, update. Kernels, drivers and libraries stay out of your Applications folder, where they belong.

x86_64, UEFI only, Secure Boot off. Free and open source.

Minimum requirements

  • CPU: 64-bit x86_64, 2 cores
  • Firmware: UEFI (Secure Boot off)
  • RAM: 2 GB (4 GB recommended)
  • Disk: 20 GB (SSD recommended)
  • USB stick: 4 GB or more
  • Internet: needed during installation
